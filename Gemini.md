This file outlines the collaborative process for refining the Vision Document. We will proceed section by section, proposing revised versions of the text.

Our primary goal is to strike the right balance between conciseness and clarity. The vision must be accessible to a broad audience, including business-minded stakeholders, not just technical experts.

To achieve this, we will follow a "first principles" approach for key concepts:
1.  **Introduce the Concept:** State the technical term.
2.  **Define it Simply:** Provide a clear, one-sentence definition.
3.  **Use an Analogy:** Make the concept intuitive with a powerful, non-technical analogy.

The aim is to be as concise as possible *without sacrificing the clarity* needed to bring every reader along.
